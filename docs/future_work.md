# Future Roadmap: Scaling the Probabilistic Engine

## 1. Multi-Asset Cross-Correlation
*   **Expansion:** Integrate a fourth stress_field focused on **Correlation Clusters**.
*   **Objective:** Identify systemic "locking" where all assets begin to move in unison, signaling a liquidity-driven structural failure across the entire market.

## 2. Dynamic Baseline Calibration
*   **Expansion:** Replace static historical baselines (`baseline_vol`) with a self-correcting long-horizon window (e.g., 5-year rolling).
*   **Objective:** Allow the system to adapt to generational shifts in volatility stress_fields without manual recalibration.

## 3. Real-Time Dashboard Integration
*   **Expansion:** Connect the `Layer3Output` Pydantic streams to a WebSocket-driven visualization dashboard.
*   **Objective:** Provide institutional risk desks with real-time "Stress-Field Heatmaps" and uncertainty evolution timelines.

## 4. Multi-Layer Convergence (CRIS Integration)
*   **Expansion:** Wire Layer 3 (Structural Stress) into Layer 2 (Macro/MMAD) and Layer 1 (Technical Signals).
*   **Objective:** Achieve "True Convergence" where a structural interpretation is either confirmed or denied by macroeconomic reality and technical price action.
