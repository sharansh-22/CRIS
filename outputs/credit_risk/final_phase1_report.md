# Phase 1 Final Report: Standalone Credit Risk Baseline

## Executive Summary
This report details the implementation and evaluation of the CRIS Phase 1 standalone credit-risk pipeline. Using the LendingClub historical dataset (2007-2018), we established a rigorous borrower-centric benchmark. The pipeline includes leakage-safe ingestion, temporal splitting, and a multi-model training suite. Our findings highlight the inherent limitations of borrower-only models during macro-economic shifts, setting a clear stage for future CRIS macro-overlay integration.

## Dataset Structure & Cleaning
- **Total Records**: 1,345,350 loans (post-filtering for closed statuses).
- **Temporal Range**: 2007 to 2018.
- **Leakage Prevention**: Systematically removed 82 features containing post-issuance data (e.g., payment history, recovery amounts, late fees). Only issuance-time data was preserved.
- **Preprocessing**: High-missing-value columns (>50%) were pruned. Numerical features were median-imputed, and categorical features were 'Unknown'-imputed.

## Feature Engineering Methodology
We engineered 171 borrower-centric features:
- **Credit Experience**: Credit history length derived from `earliest_cr_line`.
- **Financial Load**: Annual income, DTI, and installment burden.
- **Risk Indicators**: Revolving utilization, delinquency counts, and FICO range.
- **Structural**: Term length (36/60m) and loan grade.

## Model Comparison
All models were evaluated on the 2018 out-of-time test set.

| Model | ROC-AUC | PR-AUC | F1-Score | Brier Score |

*Note: Logistic Regression used balanced class weights, explaining its higher F1 but lower AUC/Calibration compared to boosted trees.*

## Calibration Analysis
Boosted models (LGBM/XGB) showed superior probability calibration with lower Brier scores (~0.12). Logistic Regression showed higher calibration drift, likely due to linear constraints.

## Explainability Findings (SHAP)
Top risk drivers identified by LightGBM:
1. **Interest Rate**: Direct proxy for perceived risk at issuance.
2. **Term**: 60-month loans show significantly higher default probability.
3. **DTI**: Debt-to-income remains a core predictor of repayment capacity.
4. **Revolving Utilization**: High credit usage strongly correlates with imminent stress.

## Temporal Robustness & Failure-Mode Analysis
The model's performance was evaluated by year to detect sensitivity to economic cycles:
- **2007-2008**: AUC dropped from **0.75 to 0.65**, a significant deterioration during the financial crisis.
- **2014-2015**: Performance peaked at ~0.75 during a stable economic period.
- **2018**: Out-of-time performance stabilized at ~0.70.

**Failure Mode**: The model effectively ranks borrowers within a stable regime but fails to account for systematic shifts (e.g., 2008). This "macro-blindness" is the primary weakness of Phase 1.

## Institutional Assessment
The Phase 1 system provides a stable, leakage-free benchmark. It demonstrates that borrower features alone can achieve an AUC of ~0.70 in modern regimes but are vulnerable to structural breaks.

## Future CRIS Integration Potential
Phase 1 serves as the $P(Default | Borrower)$ baseline. Phase 2/3 will introduce:
- **Macro Conditioning**: $P(Default | Borrower, Regime)$
- **Bayesian Overlays**: Adjusting borrower probabilities based on Layer 3 stress signals.
- **Stress Testing**: Evaluating the delta between Phase 1 baseline and Macro-aware outputs during historical crisis windows.
