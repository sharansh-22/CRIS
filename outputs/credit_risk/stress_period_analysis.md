# Stress Period Analysis
Focus on 2008 and 2018 instability windows.

## 2008 Financial Crisis
In 2008, the Macro Stress Score peaked at 0.75.
* **Governance Response:** System spent 42.1% of the year in DEFENSIVE state.
* **Approval Contraction:** CRIS reduced approval rates from baseline 93.5% to 28.2%.

## 2018 Transition
During the 2018 deterioration:
* **Detection:** CRIS identified trajectory degradation early in Q1 2018.
* **Review Burden:** Peak monthly review request volume hit 18495 loans.
