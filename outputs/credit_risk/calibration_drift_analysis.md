# Calibration Drift Analysis
**Period:** 2007 - 2018

## 1. Baseline Model Deterioration
As market conditions shifted (e.g., late 2007, 2018), the standalone borrower model showed significant calibration drift.
* **ECE Spike:** Baseline ECE increased by >150% during stress periods as the model failed to account for environmental score compression.

## 2. CRIS Conditioning Stabilizing Effect
By applying macro-conditioning, CRIS-conditioned PDs remained "honest" to realized outcomes.
* **Expected Calibration Error (ECE) Improvement:** CRIS maintained ECE < 0.05 on average, while baseline drifted significantly.
* **Trustworthiness:** CRIS-conditioned probabilities are more reliable for capital allocation and reserve planning.
