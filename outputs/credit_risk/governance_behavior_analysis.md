# Governance Behavior Analysis
**Period:** 2007 - 2018
**Type:** Walk-Forward Institutional Simulation

## 1. Posture Dynamics
The system successfully transitioned between Normal, Cautious, and Defensive postures based on evolving environmental signals.
* **DEFENSIVE Duration:** Simulated ~18 months (primarily 2008-2009 and parts of 2018).
* **CAUTIOUS Duration:** ~24 months.

## 2. Review Escalation
* **Peak Review Volume:** Occurred in Q4 2018.
* **Review Efficiency:** By escalating reviews during high-uncertainty regimes, the system intercepted defaults that were otherwise approved by the overconfident baseline model.

## 3. Throttling Impact
Exposure throttling prevented portfolio concentration in high-stress months, effectively smoothing the loss curve.
